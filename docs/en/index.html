A FRAME OF YOUR OWN

Less improvising.
More control.

From the camera image to the output for your calls: adjustments brought together in a GTK application, made for Linux.

01

Find your frame

Choose a 65°, 78° or 90° field of view on the Brio 500. Adjust digital zoom, crop, position and mirroring — or let local automatic framing follow your face.

02

Choose what is visible

Blur the room or use a PNG or JPEG as your background. Segmentation happens on this computer. Your video is never sent to a processing service.

03

Bring it to your calls

Save your settings in profiles and send the image to a virtual camera with v4l2loopback. Select that output in applications that support V4L2 cameras.

YOUR COMPUTER IS THE STUDIO

Your computer
does the work.

Enquadra processes framing and backgrounds locally. Your chosen meeting application controls video transmission. This website has no analytics, tracking or access to your camera.

  • Local processingYuNet for face detection; PP-HumanSeg for background segmentation. The models are included with the project.
  • Controls your camera providesImage, exposure and focus adjustments are available according to the V4L2 controls reported by the device.
  • Open to understand and contributeLGPL-3.0-or-later application. Models licensed under MIT and Apache-2.0. Source, limitations and instructions in the repository.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST CALL

Good to know.

Does it work with any camera?

Hardware validation was performed with the Logitech Brio 500 (046d:0943). Other V4L2 cameras may expose different controls and are not promised compatible yet. Ubuntu 24.04 also awaits hardware validation.

Is this Logitech RightSight?

Enquadra automatic framing runs in local software, using face detection and digital cropping. It is not the camera’s native RightSight. HDR and proprietary controls are not provided.

What about desk rotation (Show Mode)?

Automatic rotation when tilting the Brio was observed by the user as firmware behavior. Enquadra does not offer a proprietary control to turn Show Mode on or off.

Why do I need a virtual camera?

It makes the cropped image and selected background available to other applications. Output uses v4l2loopback, a kernel module that needs configuration. Your calling application must accept the V4L2 camera and may require its own permission.

What does alpha mean?

This release is still in development and may have bugs or behavioral changes. Validation is limited to the hardware and system listed here. Check the release notes and report issues in the repository.
